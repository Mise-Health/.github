### From scattered paperwork to structured records

We are building MISE for the people who keep a household's health paperwork in order: parents, carers, partners, and adult children helping their families.

MISE brings medical paperwork, appointment logistics, medication records, and health expense receipts into one place, designed for privacy and administrative control.

- **Family records:** organize documents and records for the people you care for.
- **Paperwork and search:** keep letters, prescriptions, and receipts structured and easier to find.
- **Appointments and medication records:** keep household schedules and recorded details together.
- **Health expenses:** organize receipts and summaries before preparing claims or speaking with a tax advisor.

### Clear boundaries

MISE is an administrative utility and digital record-keeping software. Automated document features support text extraction, indexing, formatting, and summaries of user-provided records. MISE does not provide medical, diagnostic, treatment, financial, accounting, or tax advice.
